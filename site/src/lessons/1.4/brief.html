  1. Read a matrix as a function. The same grid of numbers 1.3 called a table, asked a different question: not what it holds, but what it does.
  2. Find where the basis vectors land. They are the columns, and they are the whole matrix — every other vector follows from them.

  5. Stack transformations. Composition is one matrix, order matters, and a stack with nothing between its layers is a single layer in disguise.
