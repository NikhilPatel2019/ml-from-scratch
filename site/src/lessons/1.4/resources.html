Watch and read

  • 3Blue1Brown, Essence of Linear Algebra — ch. 3, "Linear transformations and matrices" (~11 min). If you watched this during 1.3 as suggested, watch it again: it will land differently now that you have a reason to want it. One sentence to take away — the columns of the matrix are where the basis vectors land. This lesson is that sentence, seven times.
  • 3Blue1Brown — ch. 4, "Matrix multiplication as composition" (~10 min). Watch it before exercise 6 rather than after. It animates the point the exercise makes: doing one transformation and then another is itself a transformation, so a single matrix does the pair — and the order of the product reads backwards from the order of the actions.

Reference, when you hit it

  • numpy.matmul. Go here when a shape error will not resolve. The part about how @ treats a 1-D argument explains why M @ v works without you turning v into a column first.
  • numpy.radians. Needed for exercise 5. You used its opposite in 1.2, when an angle had to come back out in degrees.

After the exercises pass

  • Your own stretch.py output. A stack of layers collapsed into one, and then the same stack with tanh between the layers refusing to collapse. Those two numbers are the argument for activation functions, measured rather than asserted.
  • 3Blue1Brown — "But what is a neural network?" (~19 min). Only once exercise 7 passes. What he draws as circles and arrows is a matrix, a bias and a squashing function, and you will have written two of the three. This is the video that makes the diagrams stop being decoration.

Deliberately not yet

  • Determinants, inverses, eigenvectors, change of basis. Chapters 5 to 14 of the same series, and all of them are about what a transformation does that a picture of the basis cannot show. Inverses and rank are 1.5; eigenvectors come back in phase 3 where they earn their keep.
  • Backpropagation. You now have the forward pass. Going backwards needs derivatives (1.6) and the chain rule (1.8), and doing it before those is how people end up memorising a formula they cannot debug.
