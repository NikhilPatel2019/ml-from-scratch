Passing tests is the floor, not the finish. This is the part that decides whether any of it stays with you.

You are done when

Not when the tests pass — when you can answer these out loud, without looking anything up:

  1. Give me two vectors that are far apart but point in the same direction. What does distance say about them? What does the angle say? Which answer is "right"?
  2. Why is distance the magnitude of something? Of what, exactly?
  3. What does a·b = |a||b|cos θ become when both vectors have length 1, and why does a vector database care?
  4. Describe the projection of a onto b in words, without the formula. Then explain why doubling b does not change it.
  5. What is the rejection, and what is guaranteed about it?
  6. Why is if dot == 0 wrong? What would go wrong with a tolerance of 1e-30? Of 0.1?
  7. You are building search over product descriptions. Distance or angle? Now over sensor readings from a machine. Which one, and why did the answer change?

If one of those makes you hesitate, the fix is not to re-read this page. Go back to the demo, change the numbers until the output does what you expected, and try again.

Write your notes

notes.md in the lesson folder holds the same questions. Answer them there in your own words — not copied from here. The things I got wrong section at the bottom will be the most valuable thing in your repository six months from now.
