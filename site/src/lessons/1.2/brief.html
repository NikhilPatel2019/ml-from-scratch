Maths introduced Distance, angle, projection, rejection
Exercises 7 + 1 stretch
You will be able to Choose distance or angle on purpose
  1. Measure distance two ways. By hand and vectorised, and see that it is the magnitude you already wrote, applied to the gap.
  2. Know when distance and angle disagree. And which one you actually wanted, for text, for coordinates, and for embeddings.
  3. Read the identity that connects them. a·b = |a||b|cos θ, three ways, one of which you already derived without knowing it.
  4. Split a vector along a direction. Projection and rejection — the move underneath least squares, PCA and attention.
  5. Stop trusting floating point. Two silent failures, both of which bite on the easiest possible input.
