Assumes 1.1 and 1.2 — dot, magnitude, normalise, distance
Maths introduced Shape, axis, broadcasting, transpose
Exercises 7 + 1 stretch
You will be able to Score a whole library in one operation
  1. Read a shape before you read the values. Almost every numpy bug is a shape bug, and shapes are small enough to see at a glance.
  2. Collapse an axis on purpose. The axis you name is the one that disappears — derive the rest rather than memorising it.
  3. Apply the broadcasting rule. Line the shapes up from the right; equal, or one of them is 1.
  4. Spot the bug that says nothing. Why a missing keepdims is silent on a square matrix and loud on a rectangular one.
  5. Do it to the whole table at once. Every pairwise dot product, every pairwise distance, and a whole library ranked in one operation.
