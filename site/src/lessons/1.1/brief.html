Assumes Nothing. First lesson.
Maths introduced Vectors, dot product, magnitude, cosine
Exercises 7 + 1 stretch
You will be able to Build a working search engine
  1. Represent anything as a vector. Why a list of numbers can stand in for a house, a customer or a word, and what "4096-dimensional" actually means.
  2. Compute and read a dot product. By hand, then vectorised — and explain what a result of zero tells you about two things.
  3. Measure similarity properly. Find the flaw in the raw dot product, fix it with cosine similarity, and understand why the fix works.
  4. Build a working search engine. Rank a small library by meaning — the mechanism behind semantic search and every RAG system.
  5. Think in arrays, not loops. The mental shift NumPy demands, and the memory-layout reason it is roughly 200× faster.
