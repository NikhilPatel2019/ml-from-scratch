This matters more than the list

Watching is not learning, and neither is dragging the arrows above. Both produce a powerful sensation of understanding, because the animation performs the hard cognitive work on your behalf. That sensation is not the same as being able to reproduce the idea, and the gap between them is where most self-taught attempts die — people watch fifty hours of excellent video, feel educated, and cannot write twenty lines of NumPy.

The test after each video: close the tab and explain the idea out loud without the animation. Budget roughly 20% of your time on video and demos, and 80% on the exercises.
