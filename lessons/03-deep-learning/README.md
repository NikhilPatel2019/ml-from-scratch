# Phase 3 — Deep Learning

*build the engine before you drive it*

You will write your own automatic differentiation engine — roughly 150 lines that explain the whole of PyTorch — and train a network on it before touching a framework.

**Milestone:** An autograd engine you wrote yourself, training a network you designed yourself.

---

## Lessons

| | Lesson | What it covers | |
|---:|---|---|---|
| `3.1` | The perceptron, and why linear models fail | XOR: the four data points that stalled an entire field for a decade. | planned |
| `3.2` | Multi-layer networks | Activations, hidden layers, and the universal approximation theorem in plain language. | planned |
| `3.3` | Backpropagation by hand | Derive it on a three-neuron network, on paper, until there is no magic left in it. | planned |
| `3.4` | Build an autograd engine | The crown jewel of this phase. A computation graph, a backward pass, and everything PyTorch does, in miniature. | planned |
| `3.5` | Train a network on your own engine | Your code, your gradients, a real classification problem, and a loss curve that goes down. | planned |
| `3.6` | PyTorch | Tensors, autograd, nn.Module and the canonical training loop — now with nothing left to take on faith. | planned |
| `3.7` | Optimisers | SGD, momentum, RMSProp, Adam — and precisely what problem each one was invented to fix. | planned |
| `3.8` | Making deep networks trainable | Initialisation, vanishing and exploding gradients, batch normalisation and layer normalisation. | planned |
| `3.9` | Regularisation in deep learning | Dropout, weight decay, early stopping and augmentation — keeping a large model honest. | planned |
| `3.10` | Convolutional networks | Build one from scratch, understand what a filter learns, then classify real images. | planned |
| `3.11` | Transfer learning | Why almost nobody trains from scratch any more, and how to fine-tune responsibly. | planned |
| `3.12` | Sequence models | RNNs and LSTMs, and a precise account of why they break on long sequences. | planned |
| `3.13` | Embeddings | word2vec, and representation learning as the central idea of modern artificial intelligence. | planned |
| `3.14` | Training at scale | GPUs, batching, mixed precision and checkpointing — the engineering that makes big models possible. | planned |
