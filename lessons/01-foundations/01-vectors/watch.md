# Watch alongside 1.1 — Vectors and the dot product

Ordered. Do not binge the whole playlist — the point is one idea, then code it.

## Before the exercises (~1h 20m)

- [ ] **3Blue1Brown, Essence of Linear Algebra — Ch 1, "Vectors"** (~10 min)
      https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDPD3MizzM2xVFitgF8hE_ab
      The geometric picture I deliberately withheld. Watch for the three views of a
      vector: physics (an arrow), CS (a list of numbers), and maths (either, who cares).
      You have the CS view already. This adds the other two.

- [ ] **3Blue1Brown — Ch 2, "Linear combinations, span, and basis vectors"** (~10 min)
      Sets up why the numbers in a vector are *coordinates* rather than just values.
      Pays off in 1.4 and again, hugely, in 2.12 (PCA).

- [ ] **Keith Galli, "Complete Python NumPy Tutorial"** (~1 hr)
      https://www.youtube.com/@KeithGalli  ·  notebook: https://github.com/KeithGalli/NumPy
      Purely practical. Watch with the notebook open and type along — do not just watch.
      You can stop after the "reshaping" section; the file-loading part can wait.

## After the exercises pass

- [ ] **3Blue1Brown — Ch 9, "Dot products and duality"** (~14 min)
      Only once your code works. The first half explains geometrically why the dot
      product measures agreement — exactly what you implemented.
      **The second half ("duality") is genuinely hard and you do not need it yet.**
      If it stops making sense around the halfway mark, that is expected. Note where
      you lost it, move on, come back in Phase 3.

## How to watch (this matters more than the list)

**Watching is not learning.** A 3Blue1Brown video produces a strong feeling of
understanding, because the animation does the hard cognitive work for you. That
feeling is not the same as being able to reproduce the idea. The exercises are the
real lesson; video is scaffolding.

The test after each video: **close it and explain the idea out loud, without the
animation.** If you can't, rewatch. If you can, move on.

Rewatch Ch 1 *after* your code passes. It is a different video the second time.
